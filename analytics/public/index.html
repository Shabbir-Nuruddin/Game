Monetization not tracked — free game, no purchases or ads

These are the revenue KPIs from the standard game-analytics framework, shown as a placeholder for the shape of the model rather than as anything to act on. They light up once the game has purchases, ads, or paid installs — which would mean adding purchase / ad_impression events (for ARPU, ARPPU, conversion, LTV) and an install source tag (for CPI). The rule the whole model rests on: LTV must exceed CPI, or buying players loses money on every install.
