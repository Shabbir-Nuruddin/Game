PRIVACY POLICY - TRUST ISSUES

Effective date and last updated: 21 August 2026

Trust Issues is published by Shabbir Nuruddin ("we", "us", or "our"). Contact: nuruddinshabbir3@gmail.com.

1. WHAT THE GAME DOES NOT REQUIRE

The Game does not require an account, real name, email address, phone number, payment information, precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, or access to personal photos and files. The current release does not display advertisements or offer in-app purchases.

2. INFORMATION STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

The Game stores progress, unlocked floors, deaths, checkpoints, achievements, best runs, Blood Shards, cosmetic ownership, equipped items, challenge codes, audio and visual preferences, a randomly generated safe nickname, and daily-play streaks using local app storage. This data normally remains until you clear the Game's storage or uninstall it, subject to Android backup settings.

3. INFORMATION SENT OFF YOUR DEVICE

Gameplay analytics: This release collects anonymous gameplay analytics so we can find the floors that are unfair and fix them. We collect gameplay events (session start, mode chosen, floor started, floor completed, checkpoint reached, death including cause, time on floor and location, run ended, pause, resume, and a periodic heartbeat), a randomly generated identifier created on first launch that tells one device's sessions apart, and basic technical context (app version, platform, screen size).

That identifier is NOT the Android Advertising ID and is not linked to any account, name, or email. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, or precise location, and the Game contains no advertising SDKs.

Data is sent over HTTPS to our own analytics service and stored in a database we control. It is used only to improve the Game's difficulty and stability. It is not sold and not shared with third parties.

Turning it off: open Settings > Analytics inside the Game and switch them off. The Game then stops sending gameplay events entirely.

Deletion: email nuruddinshabbir3@gmail.com with the random device identifier shown in Settings and we will delete the data associated with your device.

Leaderboards and death echoes: When active, the Game uploads your score, the floor reached, your generated in-game nickname, and the location on the floor where you died, so other players can see ranked scores and tombstones. Nicknames are generated by the Game and cannot be freely typed, so they contain no personal information.

Live multiplayer: This is the only feature that sends information off your device. When you choose to host or join a race, the Game connects to Photon, operated by Exit Games GmbH. Photon processes the room code, the automatically generated nickname, network and connection information, and the real-time gameplay state needed for the match. The other player in the room receives relevant movement, score, and match actions. Photon may process IP addresses under its own privacy policy. If you never open multiplayer, the Game makes no connection of this kind.

Device services: Optional story narration uses the Android Text-to-Speech service. Depending on the installed provider, narration may be processed locally or by that provider. Sharing uses Android's share sheet and the app you select handles the shared material.

4. PURPOSES AND PROVIDERS

We process information only to run a live multiplayer match when you start one, and to keep your progress on your own device. Photon provides the multiplayer networking and may process information in other countries under its own privacy policy. We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

5. RETENTION

Anonymous analytics events are kept for up to 24 months, after which they are deleted or irreversibly aggregated into totals that cannot be traced to a device. Leaderboard and death-echo records persist while the feature is live and are deleted on request.

Photon holds match and connection information under its own retention schedule; we do not keep a copy. Everything else lives on your device and remains until you clear the Game's storage, overwrite it, or uninstall the Game.

6. YOUR CHOICES AND RIGHTS

You can switch analytics off in Settings > Analytics, avoid multiplayer entirely and play the whole Game offline, mute narration in Settings, clear local data through Android Settings, or uninstall the Game.

We hold anonymous analytics data on our own service, so there IS something on our side you can ask about. To access or delete it, email nuruddinshabbir3@gmail.com with the random device identifier shown in Settings. Depending on where you live, you may have additional access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, objection, consent-withdrawal, or complaint rights.

7. CHILDREN

Trust Issues is not directed to children under 13 and should not be used below the minimum digital-consent age that applies where the player lives without permission from a parent or guardian. If you believe a child submitted information in violation of law, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where appropriate.

8. SECURITY AND CHANGES

Analytics events are sent over HTTPS and stored in an access-controlled database. Multiplayer traffic uses Photon's secure transport where supported. No method is completely secure. We may update this policy when the Game, providers, or legal requirements change, and the date above identifies the latest version. If a future version begins collecting information that this version does not, we will update this policy and the store listing before that version is released.
