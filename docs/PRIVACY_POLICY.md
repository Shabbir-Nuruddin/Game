# Privacy Policy — Trust Issues

**Effective date and last updated: 6 August 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game **Trust Issues** (the “Game”)
handles information. The Game is published by **Shabbir Nuruddin**, an
independent developer (“we”, “us”, or “our”).

Contact for privacy questions, access requests, objections, or deletion
requests: **nuruddinshabbir3@gmail.com**

## 1. Summary

Trust Issues does not require an account, real name, email address, phone
number, payment information, precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, or
access to personal photos and files.

The Game does transmit limited gameplay and online-feature data. This includes
a randomly generated installation identifier, gameplay events, and a nickname
generated by the Game from a fixed vocabulary. Generated nicknames, scores, and
death echoes can be shown to other players. Players cannot enter a custom
nickname in the current release.

The current release does not display advertisements and does not offer in-app
purchases.

## 2. Information stored only on your device

The Game stores the following information locally using Unity/Android app
preferences:

- progress, unlocked floors, deaths, checkpoints, achievements, and best runs;
- Blood Shards, cosmetic ownership, and equipped cosmetic items;
- saved custom-level codes and challenge links;
- difficulty, controls, audio, narration, haptics, accessibility, and visual
  settings;
- your generated nickname and daily-play streak; and
- a queue of unsent analytics events when analytics is enabled.

This local information normally remains until you clear the Game's storage or
uninstall it. Android or a device-backup service may retain or restore app data
according to your device and Google account settings.

## 3. Information transmitted off your device

### 3.1 Gameplay analytics

The Game sends gameplay events to our game service. These may include:

- a random identifier created for this installation;
- a random session identifier;
- app version and event timestamp;
- game mode, floor/stage, deaths, score, checkpoint, and run outcome;
- gameplay position, timing, selected options, and the cause of a death; and
- the current in-game nickname for certain events.

We use this information to understand failed flows, balance unfair levels,
measure feature use, and improve the Game. We do not use it for advertising or
to build an advertising profile.

### 3.2 Leaderboards and death echoes

The Game sends leaderboard results and death-echo records to our game service.
A record can contain the nickname, game mode, floor, score or death count,
death location, death cause, date, and random installation identifier. The
installation identifier is used to avoid showing a player their own death echo;
it is not displayed publicly.

Other players may see your nickname, score, and a short description of where or
how your character died. These online features currently operate separately
from the **ANONYMOUS DATA** setting.

### 3.3 Live multiplayer

When you host or join a live race, the Game connects to **Photon**, operated by
Exit Games GmbH. Photon processes the room code, nickname, connection and
network information, and real-time gameplay state needed to run the match. Your
nickname, movement, score, and match actions are shared with players in that
room. Photon may process network identifiers such as an IP address under its
own privacy terms: <https://www.photonengine.com/en-US/Legal/Privacy>

### 3.4 Device services you choose to use

- **Narration:** On Android, optional story narration uses the device's
  Text-to-Speech service. Depending on the voice/provider installed on the
  device, narration text may be processed locally or by that provider. You can
  mute VOICE in Settings.
- **Sharing:** If you press a share button, the Game creates the requested text
  or screenshot and passes it to Android's share sheet. The app you select then
  handles it under that app's privacy policy. Trust Issues does not upload the
  screenshot to our server.

### 3.5 Technical network information

Like most internet services, our hosting providers and Photon necessarily
receive technical request information such as IP address, timestamps, and
connection metadata. We do not intentionally store IP addresses in the Game's
analytics, leaderboard, or death-echo database, but infrastructure providers
may retain security and access logs under their own policies.

## 4. Why we process information

We process information to:

- provide requested gameplay, multiplayer, leaderboard, and death-echo
  functionality;
- save progress and preferences;
- secure, operate, troubleshoot, and improve the Game;
- understand level difficulty and feature performance; and
- comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Use.

Where applicable, our legal bases are performance of the service you request,
our legitimate interests in operating and improving the Game, consent where
required, and compliance with law. You may turn off optional analytics at any
time.

## 5. Service providers and disclosures

We use service providers only for operating the Game:

- **Render** hosts our game and analytics web service;
- **Neon** hosts its database; and
- **Photon / Exit Games GmbH** provides live multiplayer networking.

These providers may process information in countries other than yours. We may
also disclose information when required by law, to protect users and the Game,
or as part of a business transfer. We do not sell personal information, rent it
to data brokers, or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

## 6. Public information

Generated nicknames, scores, multiplayer actions, and death echoes are designed
to be visible to other players. The current release does not provide a field
for players to enter a custom nickname or message.

## 7. Retention

- Analytics events are currently retained while they are reasonably needed to
  operate, balance, and improve the Game, or until a valid deletion request is
  completed. We may retain aggregated statistics that no longer identify an
  installation.
- Death-echo lists are pruned to the most recent records as newer echoes arrive;
  older records on inactive floors may remain until deletion or the feature is
  discontinued.
- Leaderboard scores are retained while the leaderboard operates or until a
  valid deletion request is completed.
- Local app data remains until it is cleared, overwritten, or the Game is
  uninstalled, subject to device-backup behavior.
- Infrastructure providers may retain security logs according to their own
  retention schedules.

## 8. Your choices and rights

You can:

- avoid live multiplayer and leaderboard participation;
- clear local data through **Android Settings → Apps → Trust Issues → Storage →
  Clear data**, or uninstall the Game; and
- request access to or deletion of server-side information by emailing
  **nuruddinshabbir3@gmail.com**.

For a server-data request, include the nickname used, approximate dates of play,
and enough detail to locate the relevant record. Do not send passwords or
identity documents unless we specifically explain why they are legally needed.
We may need to verify that a request relates to you. We aim to complete valid
requests within 30 days, or within the period required by applicable law.

Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to correction,
restriction, portability, objection, withdrawal of consent, or complaint to a
data-protection authority. Withdrawal does not affect processing already
lawfully completed.

## 9. Children

Trust Issues is **not directed to children under 13** and should not be used by
anyone below the minimum digital-consent age that applies where they live
without permission from a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect data
from a child in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child submitted
information, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where appropriate.

## 10. Security

Game traffic uses encrypted HTTPS/TLS or secure Photon transport where
supported. We limit the information collected and restrict access to the
analytics dashboard. No storage or transmission method is completely secure,
so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game, providers, or legal requirements
change. The date at the top identifies the latest version. Material changes
will be presented through an appropriate notice before they take effect where
required.
