# Privacy Policy — Trust Issues

**Last updated: 28 July 2026**

This policy explains what Trust Issues ("the game", "we") collects when you play,
why, and what you can do about it. It is written to satisfy Google Play's User
Data policy and to be readable by an actual human.

If you only read one line: **the game does not ask for your name, your email,
your phone number, your contacts, your location, or your photos, and it has no
adverts and no in-app purchases.**

## 2. What the game stores on your device

All of the following stays **on your phone**. It is never uploaded anywhere and
it is deleted when you uninstall the game.

| What | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Your progress (floor reached, deaths, best runs) | So you can continue where you left off |
| Blood Shards balance and shop purchases | The in-game currency; cosmetics only |
| Your chosen skin, difficulty and control layout | So your settings persist |
| Audio, voice, haptics and control preferences | So your settings persist |
| The nickname you type for multiplayer | So you don't retype it every race |

This is stored using Android's standard app-preferences storage.

## 3. What the game sends off your device

### 3.1 Gameplay analytics

The game sends anonymous gameplay events (for example: "a player died on floor 4
at this position", "a run started", "a floor was cleared") to a private server
run by the developer. This is used for one purpose: **finding levels that are
unfairly hard and fixing them.**

These events include a randomly generated ID for your install, the floor and
position of the event, timings, and the nickname you chose if you set one. They
**do not** include your name, email, phone number, device identifiers such as
IMEI or advertising ID, or your location.

The random install ID is not linked to you as a person and cannot be used to
identify you. If you reinstall the game, a new one is generated.

### 3.2 Multiplayer

If — and only if — you start or join a multiplayer race, the game connects to
**Photon** (Exit Games GmbH) to pass your position and score to the other player
in real time. The nickname you chose is visible to the player you are racing.
Nothing else is shared. Photon's own privacy policy applies to that connection:
<https://www.photonengine.com/en-US/Legal/Privacy>

### 3.3 Leaderboards and death echoes

If you appear on a leaderboard, or if another player finds the spot where you
died, your chosen **nickname**, floor and score are visible to other players.
Choose a nickname you're happy for strangers to see. Nothing else about you is
shared.

## 4. What the game does NOT do

- No adverts, and no advertising or tracking SDKs
- No in-app purchases, and no payment details of any kind
- No access to contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files, or location
- No account, no sign-in, no password
- Your data is never sold, rented, or shared with data brokers

## 5. Children

Trust Issues is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect
personal information from children. Because the game collects no personal
information from anyone, there is nothing child-specific to delete — but if you
believe a child has provided information through the game, email the address
above and it will be removed.

## 6. How long data is kept

Analytics events are kept for up to **24 months** and then deleted. On-device
data lives until you uninstall the game or clear its storage.

## 7. Your rights

You can:

- **Stop analytics** — turn off "Share anonymous gameplay data" in Settings.
- **Delete everything on your device** — Android Settings → Apps → Trust Issues
  → Storage → Clear data, or simply uninstall.
- **Request deletion of server-side data** — email the address above with the
  nickname you used and roughly when you played, and it will be deleted within
  30 days.

Depending on where you live (for example the UK/EU under UK GDPR and GDPR, or
California under CCPA) you may also have rights to access, correct, or object to
processing. Use the email above and we'll action it.

The legal basis for the analytics processing is **legitimate interest** — making
the game fair and playable — and you can withdraw from it at any time using the
Settings toggle.
